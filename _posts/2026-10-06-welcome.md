Welcome to my engineering blog.

I build production Generative AI systems, and over time I've learned that the hard
part isn't getting an LLM to produce an answer — it's getting it to produce a
*trustworthy* answer, every time, under real-world load.

Over the next few posts I'll be writing about the techniques that make that possible:

- **Hybrid guardrails** that stop LLMs from generating invalid or unsafe queries
- **Deterministic evaluation** of non-deterministic models using Pytest and Pandas
- **Reliability patterns** — failover routing, concurrency control, and rate-limit handling
- **RAG pipelines** that measurably reduce hallucination

Everything here is written at the level of *general techniques and lessons* — the ideas
you can apply in your own systems. Thanks for reading.
